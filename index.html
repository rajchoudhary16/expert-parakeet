    FAQ

    Common questions from venue operators

    A streamlined answer set helps teams understand how PulseArena supports safer, faster event flow.

    How does the assistant reduce congestion?

    It analyzes density, queue length, and route weights to suggest lighter gates, parking, and walking corridors before bottlenecks worsen.

    Can guests receive personalized alerts?

    Yes. Visitors can be grouped by audience type, such as family, VIP, accessibility, or away supporters, with relevant notifications and routing.

    Does it support emergency messaging?

    Emergency mode reroutes traffic toward safe exits, suppresses nonessential guidance, and elevates safety notices across the interface.

    Is the layout responsive and accessible?

    The interface is built with readable contrast, flexible grids, strong tap targets, and a clean hierarchy that scales across device sizes.
